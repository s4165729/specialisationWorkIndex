About the Prototypes

For this assignment i have explored three different ideas, building two parallel Prototypes for each. I built two ideas side by side for each which let me compare what kinds of different approaches i could take towards the same idea, rather than commiting to my first solution etc stright away this process let me think about this in detail on how this could work. Each prototype below links to the live version and it's full commit history and explanations.

Idea 1: Spacial Awareness using sound

This idea explores whether the position on a screen can be understood by sound alone. Instead of looking at where something is, the person moves a circle and listens, using a pitch and note changes tell them where they are in the space.

Prototype 1: Dot sound prototype

Press start then move you mouse over the field moving left and right steps through the notes c to b and moving up and down will shift the pitch up or down an octave. Together the two axes give a sound map of the box so the position of the pointer can be heard.
